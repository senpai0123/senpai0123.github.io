## 🌍 What Is little-chits?

little-chits is a simulation of a tiny world filled with small AI creatures. These creatures are not scripted puppets. They think, react, explore, and learn. One chit might find a new way to gather food. Another might copy that idea. A third might improve it, then teach it to a younger chit. Over time, the world fills with shared knowledge.

The creatures use an LLM as their thinking engine. That means their behaviour can be varied, creative, and sometimes surprising. You can watch them form habits, solve small problems, and build on what earlier creatures learned. The result is a calm, curious world that feels alive.

You do not need to know anything about AI, agents, or programming. You open little-chits, watch the creatures, and follow the stories that unfold.

## 👀 What You Can Watch

little-chits is built around watching, not managing. You can sit back and observe a living world where small events have long effects.

- 🧭 **Discovery:** A chit finds something new in its world, such as a better way to use a resource or a new place to explore.
- 🏗️ **Building:** Creatures create small structures, tools, or routines that make their lives easier.
- 🎓 **Teaching:** One chit shows another how to do something, and the knowledge spreads.
- 📚 **Knowledge passing:** Ideas survive beyond one creature. Younger chits inherit what older ones learned.
- 🌱 **Generations:** Each generation can start with more than the last one knew.
- 🧠 **LLM minds:** The creatures use an LLM to make choices, so their behaviour can feel open-ended and personal.

You can follow one creature for a while, then zoom out and watch the whole civilisation change. Some moments are quiet. Some are busy. Some are funny. The world keeps producing small stories.

## 🧠 Any LLM in Their Minds

The phrase any LLM in their minds is central to little-chits. Each creature has an LLM-powered mind that helps it decide what to do next. Instead of following a fixed list of actions, the creatures can respond to their world in more natural ways.

This makes the simulation feel less like a machine and more like a tiny society. A creature might decide to explore. Another might decide to stay and teach. Another might try something no one has tried before. The LLM gives each chit a small spark of personality.

You do not need to understand how LLMs work to enjoy little-chits. You simply watch the results. The creatures discover, build, teach, and pass knowledge on. That is the heart of the experience.
